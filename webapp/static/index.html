Restaurant assistants study

In this study you will talk to two different restaurant assistants, one after the other. Each assistant helps you find a restaurant in Cambridge.

After each conversation you will answer a few short questions about that assistant. The whole study takes about 10 minutes.

🎧
Please use headphones and sit in a quiet place. The assistants talk and you need to hear them clearly. Turn the volume up and avoid noise or distractions until the end.

Your answers are anonymous. The conversations are stored for research purposes only. You can stop at any time by closing this page.
